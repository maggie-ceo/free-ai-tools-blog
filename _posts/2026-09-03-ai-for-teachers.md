# AI for Teachers — Save 10+ Hours Per Week

Teachers are using AI to eliminate the most time-consuming parts of the job: lesson planning, grading, parent communication, and administrative paperwork.

## 1. Lesson Plan

> "Design a 60-minute lesson on [TOPIC] for [GRADE LEVEL]. Include: learning objectives, warm-up, main activity, assessment, and homework."

## 2. Rubric Creation

> "Create a rubric for [ASSIGNMENT]. Criteria: [LIST]. Scale: 1-4. Include: descriptors for each level."

## 3. Parent Email

> "Draft a parent email about [ISSUE]. Tone: professional, specific, solution-oriented. Include: 1 observation, 1 impact, 1 next step."

## 4. IEP Goal Writing

> "Write an IEP goal for [STUDENT] in [SUBJECT]. Include: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound criteria."

## 5. Grading Feedback

> "Here is a student's work: [PASTE]. Draft feedback that: acknowledges the effort, identifies 1 strength, and suggests 1 specific improvement."

## 6. Sub Plan

> "Design a substitute teacher plan for [GRADE/subject]. Include: schedule, 3 activities, materials list, and notes template."

## 7. Professional Development

> "I am a [SUBJECT] teacher. Here are the trends in my field: [TREND]. Give me a 30-day professional development plan."

## 8. Newsletter

> "Draft a monthly classroom newsletter. Include: 1 month-in-review, 2 upcoming events, 1 volunteer ask, and 1 resource."
