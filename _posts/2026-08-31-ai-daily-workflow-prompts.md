## Morning Scan (5 prompts)

Before you check email, paste your calendar + top 3 priorities into this prompt:

> "Here is my calendar for today and my top 3 priorities. Identify time blocks I should protect, meetings to skip or shorten, and any conflicts. Output: 3 bullet actions I should take before 10 AM."

**Result:** You start the day with a plan, not a reaction.

---

## Inbox Triage (5 prompts)

> "Here is my inbox. For each email, classify as FIRE / IGNORE / DELEGATE. For FIRE items, draft a 1-line reply I can send. For DELEGATE items, write a handoff note for my VA."

**Result:** Inbox zero in half the time.

---

## Planning & Execution (10 prompts)

Use these for sprint planning, task breakdown, and writing outlines. The key pattern: ask for structure first, details second.

> "I need to [TASK]. Give me a 3-level outline: phases → milestones → weekly actions. Then identify the 2 highest-leverage actions I should start today."

---

## Meeting Prep (5 prompts)

> "I have a meeting with [PERSON/ROLE] about [TOPIC]. Generate 3 questions that force a decision, and 1 question that surfaces hidden concerns. Also draft a 30-second agenda I can share in advance."

---

## End-of-Day Review (5 prompts)

> "Today I accomplished [LIST]. What did I avoid and why? Output: 1 thing to stop, 1 thing to start, 1 thing to continue."
