# AI for Content Creators — 12 Workflows for YouTube, Blogs, and Social

Content creators are using AI to eliminate the blank page problem. The key is not letting AI create for you — it is using AI to accelerate the parts that slow you down: ideation, outlining, and editing.

## 1. YouTube Video Outline

> "I am creating a YouTube video about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Generate a 10-minute video outline: hook, 5 sections, and a CTA. Include: 1 key insight per section and 1 visual suggestion."

## 2. Blog Post Outline

> "I am writing a post about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Generate a H2-level outline with 6-8 sections. For each section, give me: the core insight, one example, and one action step."

## 3. Social Media Calendar

> "Create a 30-day content calendar for [PLATFORM] for a [INDUSTRY] creator. Include: post type, caption, hashtags, and best time to post."

## 4. Hook Generator

> "I am writing about [TOPIC]. Generate 10 hooks that stop scrollers. Formats: question, bold statement, curiosity gap, personal story, and counterintuitive."

## 5. Caption Writing

> "Turn this rough idea into a [PLATFORM] caption. Tone: conversational, specific, no fluff. Max [X] characters. Include a hook in the first line and a CTA at the end."

## 6. Hashtag Research

> "I am posting about [TOPIC] on [PLATFORM]. Generate 20 hashtags: 5 broad, 10 niche, 5 ultra-niche. Include search volume estimates."

## 7. Email Newsletter

> "Draft a weekly newsletter for [INDUSTRY] audience. Include: 1 main insight, 3 links with summaries, 1 CTA. Max 500 words."

## 8. Content Repurposing

> "Turn this [BLOG POST / VIDEO TRANSCRIPT] into: 1 Twitter thread, 3 LinkedIn posts, and 1 email newsletter."

## 9. Thumbnail Ideas

> "I am creating a YouTube video about [TOPIC]. Generate 5 thumbnail concepts: focal point, text overlay, color scheme, and emotional hook."

## 10. SEO Title Options

> "I am writing about [TOPIC]. Generate 10 SEO title options. Include: power words, numbers, and the primary keyword."

## 11. Comment Response

> "Here is a comment on my [PLATFORM] post: [COMMENT]. Draft a response that: acknowledges the point, adds value, and drives engagement."

## 12. Content Audit

> "Here is my content from the last 30 days: [LIST]. Identify: top 3 performing posts, 3 patterns, and 1 content gap I should fill."
