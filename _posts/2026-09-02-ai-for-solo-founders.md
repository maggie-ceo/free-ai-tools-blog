## 10 Workflows for Solo Founders

### 1. The 5-Minute Investor Update

> "Turn these metrics into a 3-bullet investor update: win, loss, ask. Tone: concise, confident, no fluff."

### 2. Meeting Prep in 30 Seconds

> "I have a meeting with [PERSON] about [TOPIC]. Generate 3 questions that force a decision, 1 question that surfaces hidden concerns, and a 30-second agenda."

### 3. Inbox Triage

> "Here is my inbox. For each email, classify as FIRE / IGNORE / DELEGATE. For FIRE items, draft a 1-line reply."

### 4. Hiring Decision Matrix

> "Here are 3 candidates for [ROLE]. Score them 1-10 on: technical fit, culture add, growth trajectory, risk. Return as a table with a one-line recommendation."

### 5. Pricing Page Feedback

> "Review my pricing page structure. Identify: conversion blockers, missing plans, value proposition gaps, and 3 specific changes."

### 6. Weekly Review

> "This week I: [LIST ACCOMPLISHMENTS]. What did I avoid and why? Output: 1 thing to stop, 1 thing to start, 1 thing to continue."

### 7. Customer Feedback Synthesis

> "Synthesize these support tickets into: top 3 pain points, top 3 feature requests, and 1 quick win I can ship this week."

### 8. Job Description

> "Write a job description for [ROLE] at [STAGE] startup. Include: core responsibilities, required skills, nice-to-have skills, how we work, and what success looks like in 90 days."

### 9. Competitive Analysis

> "Compare [COMPANY A] and [COMPANY B] on [DIMENSION]. Output as a table with columns: Feature | A | B | Winner | Why."

### 10. Daily Prioritization

> "Here is my calendar and task list for today. Identify the 1-2 highest-leverage actions I should protect time for. Flag anything that can be delegated or deleted."
