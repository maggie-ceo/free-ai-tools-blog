# Cold Email Templates for Agencies — 10 Ready-to-Use Outreach Emails

Agencies live and die by outreach. Whether you are a marketing agency, dev shop, or design studio, the ability to consistently land new clients depends on one thing: the quality of your cold email.

Below are 10 templates designed specifically for agency outreach. Each follows the 3-layer personalization framework: observation → pain point → relevant outcome.

## 1. The Observation Opener

Subject: Quick question about [company]'s [specific observation]

> "Saw you [hired X / launched Y / posted about Z] last month — usually a sign you are expanding into [segment]. When teams scale from [X] to [Y], most [systems/processes] break down before the [outcome] does. The agencies I work with usually cut [metric] by [X%] in the first quarter. Worth a 15-minute call?"

## 2. The Audit-Style Opener

Subject: 3 quick wins on your [website/process]

> "I spent 20 minutes on your [website/process] and found 3 quick wins: [specific 1], [specific 2], [specific 3]. None require a full redesign. If useful, I can send over a 2-minute Loom walking through each."

## 3. The Case Study Drop

Subject: How [similar company] cut [metric] by [X%]

> "We helped [similar company] go from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. The key change: [specific action]. I see a similar opportunity at [prospect]. Worth a quick call?"

## 4. The Referral Ask

Subject: [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out

> "[Mutual connection] mentioned you are exploring [service area]. We recently helped [client] achieve [result]. Happy to share how it worked — no pitch, just the playbook."

## 5. The Value-First Follow-Up

Subject: New insight on [their pain point]

> "Following up on my last email. I just published a breakdown of how [similar company] solved [problem]. The key insight: [specific finding]. Thought it might be useful for [prospect]."

## 6. The Direct Question

Subject: Is [outcome] a priority right now?

> "I have reached out a few times. Is [outcome] a priority for you right now? If not, I will close your file. If yes, I have a specific idea worth 15 minutes."

## 7. The Breakup Email

Subject: Closing your file

> "I have tried to reach you a few times. I will assume the timing is not right and close your file. If you ever want to revisit [outcome], here is a link to a case study that might be useful: [link]."

## 8. The LinkedIn Connection

Subject: Connection request (no pitch)

> "Saw your post on [topic] — specifically agree with [point]. Would love to connect and follow your work in [space]."

## 9. The Post-Demo Follow-Up

Subject: Next steps from our call

> "Great speaking with you. As discussed: here is the proposal, the timeline, and the next steps. I have also attached a case study from a similar engagement."

## 10. The Re-Engagement Email

Subject: It has been a while

> "It has been [X months] since we last spoke. Since then, we have helped [similar company] achieve [result]. If [outcome] is back on your radar, happy to share how it worked."
