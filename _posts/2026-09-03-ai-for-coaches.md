# AI for Coaches — Scale Your Practice Without Burning Out

Coaches are using AI to scale their practice without burning out. The key is not replacing the coaching — it is eliminating the administrative overhead that consumes 40% of a coach's week.

## 1. Session Notes

> "Turn these rough notes from my coaching session with [CLIENT] into: 1) 3 key insights, 2) action items, 3) a 3-bullet summary for the client."

## 2. Client Onboarding

> "Design a client onboarding sequence for a [NICHE] coaching practice. Include: welcome packet, intake questionnaire, session 1 agenda, and 30-day check-in."

## 3. Marketing Content

> "I am a [NICHE] coach. Generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas that attract my ideal client. Include: hook, core insight, and CTA."

## 4. Proposal Draft

> "Draft a coaching proposal for [CLIENT]. Include: scope, timeline, pricing tiers, and 1 differentiator."

## 5. Testimonial Request

> "Draft a testimonial request email for a client who just completed a [PROGRAM]. Include: 3 specific questions, a deadline, and an easy reply format."

## 6. Program Design

> "Design a 12-week coaching program for [NICHE]. Include: weekly themes, session structure, and accountability mechanisms."

## 7. Client Check-In

> "Draft a 3-email check-in sequence for between sessions. Email 1: accountability. Email 2: resource. Email 3: next step."

## 8. Referral Ask

> "Draft a referral request email for a satisfied client. Include: 1 specific ask, an incentive, and an easy forward format."
