# AI for Recruiters — Screen Faster, Hire Better

Recruiters are using AI to eliminate the most time-consuming parts of the hiring process: resume screening, outreach, interview prep, and candidate follow-up. Below are the workflows that deliver the most leverage.

## 1. Resume Screening

> "Here are 20 resumes for [ROLE]. Score each 1-10 on: technical fit, culture add, growth trajectory, risk. Return as a table with a one-line recommendation."

## 2. Job Description

> "Write a job description for [ROLE] at [STAGE] company. Include: core responsibilities, required skills, nice-to-have skills, how we work, and what success looks like in 90 days."

## 3. Cold Outreach to Candidates

> "I am a recruiter reaching out to [CANDIDATE] for [ROLE]. Draft a 3-sentence InMail: observation about their background, the opportunity, and a specific outcome."

## 4. Interview Prep

> "I have an interview with [CANDIDATE] for [ROLE]. Generate 5 questions that assess: technical skill, culture add, problem-solving, and growth trajectory."

## 5. Reference Check

> "Draft 5 reference check questions for [CANDIDATE] who is being considered for [ROLE]. Focus: strengths, areas for growth, culture fit, and 1 specific accomplishment."

## 6. Offer Letter

> "Draft an offer letter for [CANDIDATE] for [ROLE]. Include: salary, equity, start date, and 1 personalized reason we are excited to have them."

## 7. Rejection Email

> "Draft a rejection email for [CANDIDATE] who interviewed for [ROLE]. Tone: respectful, specific, encouraging. Include: 1 specific strength and 1 reason."

## 8. Candidate Nurture

> "Draft a 3-email nurture sequence for a candidate who said no to [ROLE]. Email 1: thank you. Email 2: new opportunity. Email 3: check-in."

## 9. Hiring Manager Brief

> "Here is the interview feedback for [CANDIDATE]. Turn this into a 1-page hiring manager brief: strengths, concerns, recommendation."

## 10. Pipeline Analysis

> "Here is my recruiting pipeline: [LIST]. Flag: candidates stuck > 14 days, roles with no candidates, and 1 action per bottleneck."
