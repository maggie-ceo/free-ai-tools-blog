# How to Write Cold Email That Gets Replies (2026)

Cold email isn't dead — but bad cold email is. The difference between a 40% reply rate and a 0.5% reply rate usually comes down to three things: targeting, personalization depth, and follow-up structure.

This guide covers the framework I use for B2B outreach, plus a free template pack if you want to skip straight to execution.

## The 3-Layer Personalization Rule

**Layer 1 — Company-specific observation (first 1-2 sentences):**
Reference something specific about their business: a recent hire, a product launch, a job posting, a press mention. This proves you didn't buy a list.

Example: "Saw you hired two new account executives last month — usually a sign you're expanding into [segment]."

**Layer 2 — Pain point (1 sentence):**
Connect your observation to a problem they likely have. Don't assume — infer from the signal.

Example: "When teams scale from 5 to 20 reps, most CRMs break down before the sales process does."

**Layer 3 — Relevant outcome (1 sentence):**
What changes if they fix it? Keep it specific.

Example: "The teams I work with usually cut ramp time by 30% in the first quarter!"

## Subject Lines That Work

Short subject lines outperform long ones. The patterns:

1. **Question format:** "Quick question about [their process]"
2. **Observation:** "Saw your post on [topic]"
3. **Specific outcome:** "How [company] cut churn by 18%"
4. **Brevity:** "[First name] — [one line]"
5. **Curiosity gap:** "The $[X] mistake I see at [company]"

Avoid: "Introduction", "Following up" (unless real follow-up), "Partnership opportunity" (sounds like everyone else).

## The Follow-Up Sequence

Most replies come from follow-ups, not the first email. Here's the cadence:

- **Day 1:** Initial email
- **Day 3:** New insight or resource related to their pain point
- **Day 7:** Short case study from a similar account
- **Day 12:** Direct question — "Is this a priority right now?"
- **Day 18:** Final value drop + clear exit option
- **Day 25:** Breakup email (leaves door open)

Each follow-up should feel like a new touchpoint, not "just checking in."

## Deliverability Checklist

Even the best email fails if it doesn't land:

- Set up SPF, DKIM, DMARC (takes 30 minutes)
- Warm up your domain for 2-4 weeks before sending volume
- Keep links to 1-2 per email max
- Avoid spam trigger words: "free", "guarantee", "act now", all-caps subject lines
- Use a custom tracking domain (not the default from your ESP)
