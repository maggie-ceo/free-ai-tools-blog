# AI Prompts for Virtual Assistants — 15 Ready-to-Use Workflows

Virtual assistants are using AI to deliver better results in half the time. The difference between a good VA and a great VA in 2026 often comes down to one thing: prompt quality.

If you are a virtual assistant looking to 2x your output (and justify higher rates), these 15 prompts are designed for the tasks VAs do most.

## 1. Email Management

> "Here is my client's inbox. For each email, classify as FIRE / IGNORE / DELEGATE. For FIRE items, draft a 1-line reply in my client's voice. For DELEGATE items, write a handoff note."

## 2. Calendar Optimization

> "Here is my client's calendar for this week. Identify: time blocks to protect, meetings to skip or shorten, conflicts, and 3 actions to take before 10 AM."

## 3. Meeting Notes

> "Turn these meeting notes into: 1) 3 key decisions, 2) action items with owners, 3) a 3-bullet summary for stakeholders."

## 4. Travel Planning

> "Plan a 5-day trip to [CITY] for my client. Include: flights (economy plus or business), hotels (4-star, central), restaurant reservations, and a daily itinerary. Budget: $[X]."

## 5. Research Brief

> "Research [TOPIC] and produce a 1-page brief: key findings, 3 sources, 1 recommendation. Format as a memo."

## 6. Social Media Calendar

> "Create a 30-day content calendar for [PLATFORM] for a [INDUSTRY] business. Include: post type, caption, hashtags, and best time to post."

## 7. Client Onboarding

> "Design a client onboarding sequence for a [SERVICE] business. Include: welcome email, intake form, first-week checklist, and 30-day check-in."

## 8. Invoice Follow-Up

> "Here are 5 overdue invoices. Draft a polite follow-up email for each. Tone: professional, firm, no fluff."

## 9. Data Entry Cleanup

> "Here is a messy spreadsheet. Clean it: standardize formats, remove duplicates, flag errors, and add a summary row."

## 10. Presentation Outline

> "I need a 10-slide presentation on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Give me: title, 10 slide titles, 1 key message per slide."

## 11. Newsletter Draft

> "Draft a weekly newsletter for [INDUSTRY] audience. Include: 1 main insight, 3 links with summaries, 1 CTA. Max 500 words."

## 12. SOP Documentation

> "Turn this workflow into a standard operating procedure. Format: purpose, scope, step-by-step, troubleshooting."

## 13. Vendor Comparison

> "Compare these 3 vendors on [CRITERIA]. Output as a table with columns: Feature | Vendor A | Vendor B | Vendor C | Winner."

## 14. Customer Support Response

> "Here is a customer complaint. Draft a response that: acknowledges the issue, explains the fix, offers compensation, and prevents recurrence."

## 15. Weekly Report

> "Here is what I accomplished this week: [LIST]. Turn this into a client-ready weekly report. Format: wins, blockers, next week priorities."
