# AI for Sales Teams — Automate Outreach, Close More Deals

Sales teams are using AI to eliminate the repetitive parts of outreach: research, personalization, follow-up drafting, and pipeline analysis. The result: more calls, more demos, more closed deals.

## 1. Lead Research

> "Here is a list of 20 target accounts in [INDUSTRY]. For each, find: recent hires, funding status, product launches, and 1 personalized opening line for cold outreach. Output as CSV."

## 2. Pipeline Review

> "Here is my CRM export. Flag: deals stuck > 14 days, deals with no next action, deals where champion changed jobs. Output: top 3 risks and 1 action per risk."

## 3. Follow-Up Drafting

> "Here are 10 deals where I sent the last email > 7 days ago. For each, draft a follow-up with a new angle: new insight, case study, or direct question."

## 4. Objection Handling

> "Here are the top 5 objections I hear in demos. For each, write a 2-sentence response that acknowledges the concern and pivots to value."

## 5. Win/Loss Analysis

> "Here are my won and lost deals from the last quarter. What patterns do you see? Output: top 3 reasons we won, top 3 reasons we lost, and 1 process change."

## 6. Discovery Call Prep

> "I have a discovery call with [PROSPECT] at [COMPANY]. Generate 5 questions that uncover pain points, 3 questions that force a decision, and 1 question that surfaces hidden concerns."

## 7. Proposal Draft

> "Draft a proposal for [PROSPECT] for [SERVICE]. Include: scope, timeline, pricing tiers, and 1 differentiator."

## 8. Competitive Battlecard

> "Compare us vs [COMPETITOR] on [DIMENSION]. Output as a table with columns: Feature | Us | Competitor | Winner | Why. Include 1 objection handler for each competitor advantage."

## 9. MEDDIC Qualification

> "Here is my deal: [DEAL INFO]. Qualify it using MEDDIC: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion. Output: 1-10 score per dimension."

## 10. Churn Risk Analysis

> "Here is my customer health data. Flag: accounts at risk of churning, accounts ready for upsell, and 1 action per account."
