# AI for Students — Study Smarter, Not Harder

Students are using AI to study more efficiently: summarizing lectures, generating practice questions, and editing papers. The key is not letting AI do the work — it is using AI to accelerate the learning process.

## 1. Lecture Summary

> "Here are my lecture notes: [PASTE]. Turn this into: 1) 3 key concepts, 2) 5 bullet summary, 3) 3 practice questions."

## 2. Essay Outline

> "I am writing an essay about [TOPIC]. Generate a 5-paragraph outline: thesis, 3 arguments, counterargument, conclusion."

## 3. Study Guide

> "Here is my textbook chapter: [PASTE]. Turn this into a 1-page study guide: key terms, key concepts, and 5 practice questions."

## 4. Citation Generator

> "Here is my source: [SOURCE]. Format it in [APA/MLA/Chicago] style."

## 5. Presentation Outline

> "I need a 10-slide presentation on [TOPIC]. Give me: title, 10 slide titles, 1 key message per slide."

## 6. Research Summary

> "Here are 5 sources on [TOPIC]: [LIST]. Synthesize into a 1-page research summary: key findings, gaps, and 1 recommendation."

## 7. Paper Editing

> "Here is my draft: [PASTE]. Edit for: clarity, conciseness, tone consistency, and citation format."

## 8. Exam Prep

> "Here is my course material: [PASTE]. Generate 10 practice questions: 5 multiple choice, 3 short answer, 2 essay."
