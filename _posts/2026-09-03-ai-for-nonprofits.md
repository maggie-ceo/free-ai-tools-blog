# AI for Nonprofits — Do More With Less

Nonprofits operate with limited resources. AI is helping nonprofits write grants faster, communicate with donors more effectively, and measure impact more efficiently.

## 1. Grant Proposal

> "Draft a grant proposal for [PROJECT] for [FUNDER]. Include: executive summary, problem statement, methodology, budget narrative, and evaluation plan."

## 2. Donor Email

> "Draft a donor thank-you email. Tone: warm, specific, impactful. Include: 1 specific outcome, 1 story, and 1 next step."

## 3. Impact Report

> "Here is our data from the last year: [DATA]. Turn this into a 1-page impact report: 3 key metrics, 1 story, 1 ask."

## 4. Social Media Calendar

> "Create a 30-day content calendar for a [CAUSE] nonprofit. Include: post type, caption, hashtags, and best time to post."

## 5. Volunteer Recruitment

> "Draft a volunteer recruitment post for [EVENT/ROLE]. Include: the role, the impact, the time commitment, and 1 CTA."

## 6. Board Update

> "Turn these updates into a 1-page board update: programs, finances, challenges, and 1 ask."

## 7. Fundraising Email

> "Draft a 3-email fundraising campaign for [CAUSE]. Email 1: story. Email 2: urgency. Email 3: final ask."

## 8. Newsletter

> "Draft a monthly donor newsletter. Include: 1 impact story, 2 upcoming events, 1 volunteer spotlight, and 1 donation ask."
