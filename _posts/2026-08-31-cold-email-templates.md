## The 7 Subject Line Formulas

Open rates above 40% usually come down to the subject line. Here are the patterns that work:

1. **Question:** "Quick question about [company]'s [process]"
2. **Specific outcome:** "How [competitor] cut churn by 18%"
3. **Personal observation:** "Saw your post on [topic]"
4. **Brevity:** "[Name] — [one line]"
5. **Curiosity gap:** "The $[X] mistake I see at [company]"
6. **Social proof:** "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"
7. **Polarizing:** "Not interested in [outcome]?"

Avoid: "Introduction", "Quick question" (too vague), "Following up" (unless it's a real follow-up).

## The 32 Templates (By Persona)

### SDR / B2B Outreach
- Initial touch (company-specific observation)
- Value-first (share a resource, no ask)
- Case study drop (short, with proof)
- Partner introduction (via mutual connection)

### Agency Owner
- Audit-style (point out a gap on their site)
- Benchmark (how similar clients grew)
- Partnership invite (not a sales pitch)

### Freelancer / Consultant
- Portfolio-led (show recent work relevant to them)
- Referral ask (from past client)
- Niche positioning ("I only work with [specific vertical]")

### SaaS Founder
- Integration pitch (complementary, not competitive)
- Feedback ask (low-pressure entry point)
- Pricing page observation + improvement tip

## Follow-Up Sequence (12 Templates)

Most replies come from follow-ups, not the first email. This pack includes templates for days 3, 7, 12, 18, and 25 — each with a different angle (value add, social proof, urgency, breakup).

**Day 3:** New insight or resource related to their pain point.
**Day 7:** Short case study or result from a similar account.
**Day 12:** Direct question — "Is this a priority right now?"
**Day 18:** Final value drop + clear exit option.
**Day 25:** Breakup email (leaves the door open).
