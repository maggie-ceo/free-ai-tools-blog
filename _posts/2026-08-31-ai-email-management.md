# AI for Email Management: 5 Workflows That Save 10+ Hours

Email is the universal communication layer — and for most professionals, it's also the biggest time sink. The average worker spends 3+ hours per day on email. Below are 5 AI workflows that cut that in half without missing anything important.

## 1. Inbox Triage (FIRE / IGNORE / DELEGATE)

Paste your inbox into this prompt:
> "For each email, classify as FIRE / IGNORE / DELEGATE. For FIRE items, draft a 1-line reply. For DELEGATE items, write a handoff note."

**Time saved:** 45 min → 10 min per day.

## 2. Weekly Cleanup

> "Review my inbox from this week. Categorize into: needs-reply, reference, spam/unsubscribe. Output: a 3-bullet summary of what I missed and what I should filter going forward."

## 3. Meeting Request Filter

> "Here are 5 meeting requests in my inbox. For each, decide: accept / decline / delegate. If declining, draft a polite 1-line reason. If delegating, write the handoff note."

## 4. Follow-Up Reminder

> "Here is my sent folder from the last 14 days. Flag any threads where I sent the last message and haven't received a reply. Draft 3 follow-up options: short check-in, value-add, or polite close."

## 5. Email Analytics

> "Here is my inbox data for the last month (sender domains, subject patterns, response rates). Identify: top time-wasters, best response patterns, and 1 rule I should add to my email policy."
