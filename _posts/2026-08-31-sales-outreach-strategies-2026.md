# Sales Outreach Strategies That Work in 2026

Outreach in 2026 is not about volume — it's about signal. The teams that win are the ones that can identify a real buying signal, personalize at scale, and follow up without being annoying. Below is the framework I use for B2B sales outreach.

## The Signal-First Framework

**Step 1 — Identify the trigger:**
Look for real signals: recent hires, funding rounds, product launches, job postings, press mentions. These prove your outreach is researched, not scraped.

**Step 2 — Personalize at depth:**
Don't just use `{{first_name}}`. Reference the specific trigger and connect it to a pain point. One well-researched sentence outperforms 10 generic ones.

**Step 3 — Multi-channel orchestration:**
Email + LinkedIn + short voicemail. Each touchpoint reinforces the others. Keep messaging consistent across channels.

## Outreach Cadence That Converts

| Day | Channel | Angle |
|-----|---------|-------|
| 1 | Email | Value-first observation |
| 2 | LinkedIn | Connection request (no pitch) |
| 3 | Email | Short follow-up with new insight |
| 5 | Voicemail | 30-second reference to their pain point |
| 7 | Email | Case study or result from similar account |
| 12 | Email | Direct question — "Is this a priority?" |
| 18 | Email | Final value drop + exit option |
| 25 | Email | Breakup (leaves door open) |

## What Not to Do

- Don't send 5 follow-ups in 5 days
- Don't pitch in the first message
- Don't use "partnership opportunity" as a subject line
- Don't lie about mutual connections
- Don't buy lists — build signals
