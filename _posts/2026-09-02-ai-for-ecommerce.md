# AI for Ecommerce — Product Descriptions, Support, and Upsell

Ecommerce stores are using AI to write product descriptions faster, handle support tickets more efficiently, and increase average order value with smart upsells. Below are the workflows that deliver the most ROI.

## 1. Product Description

> "Write a product description for [PRODUCT] on [PLATFORM]. Include: 1 benefit-driven headline, 3 key features, 1 social proof element, and 1 CTA. Max 200 words."

## 2. Product Description (Bulk)

> "Here are 50 products with basic specs: [LIST]. For each, write a 100-word product description. Format: headline, 3 features, 1 benefit. Output as CSV."

## 3. Customer Support Triage

> "Here is a customer support ticket: [TICKET]. Classify as: refund, replacement, how-to, or complaint. Draft a response that: acknowledges the issue, explains the fix, and prevents recurrence."

## 4. Review Response

> "Here is a customer review: [REVIEW]. Draft a response that: thanks the customer, addresses the specific point, and reinforces the value."

## 5. Upsell Email

> "Here is a customer who purchased [PRODUCT]. Draft a 3-sentence upsell email for [COMPLEMENTARY PRODUCT]: the reason, the benefit, and a specific outcome."

## 6. Abandoned Cart Recovery

> "Draft a 3-email abandoned cart sequence for [PRODUCT]. Email 1: reminder. Email 2: social proof. Email 3: urgency + discount."

## 7. SEO Title Tags

> "Here are 20 products: [LIST]. For each, generate an SEO title tag: primary keyword + brand + key feature. Max 60 characters."

## 8. Meta Descriptions

> "Here are 20 product pages: [LIST]. For each, generate a meta description: primary keyword + benefit + CTA. Max 160 characters."

## 9. Email Subject Lines

> "I am sending a [PROMOTION / NEW PRODUCT / BACK IN STOCK] email. Generate 10 subject lines: 3 curiosity, 3 urgency, 2 social proof, 2 benefit-driven."

## 10. Customer Segmentation

> "Here is my customer purchase data. Segment into: VIP, at-risk, one-time, and loyal. For each segment, suggest 1 email campaign."
