# 10 AI Productivity Hacks for Founders

Founders wear every hat: product, sales, support, hiring, investor updates. The constraint isn't time — it's context switching. Below are 10 AI workflows that cut decision fatigue and reclaim 5-10 hours per week.

## 1. The 5-Minute Investor Update

Every week, paste your key metrics into this prompt:
> "Turn these metrics into a 3-bullet investor update: win, loss, ask. Tone: concise, confident, no fluff."

**Time saved:** 30 minutes → 5 minutes per week.

## 2. Meeting Prep in 30 Seconds

Before any important meeting:
> "I have a meeting with [PERSON] about [TOPIC]. Generate 3 questions that force a decision, 1 question that surfaces hidden concerns, and a 30-second agenda I can share in advance."

**Time saved:** 15 minutes → 30 seconds per meeting.

## 3. Inbox Triage Without Reading Every Email

> "Here is my inbox. For each email, classify as FIRE / IGNORE / DELEGATE. For FIRE items, draft a 1-line reply. For DELEGATE items, write a handoff note."

**Time saved:** 45 minutes → 10 minutes per day.

## 4. Hiring Decision Matrix

When comparing candidates:
> "Here are 3 candidates for [ROLE] with their backgrounds. Score them 1-10 on: technical fit, culture add, growth trajectory, risk. Return as a table with a one-line recommendation."

**Time saved:** 2 hours → 20 minutes per hire.

## 5. Pricing Page Feedback

When you're stuck on pricing:
> "Review my pricing page structure. Identify: conversion blockers, missing plans, value proposition gaps, and 3 specific changes that would improve upgrades."

**Time saved:** 1 hour → 10 minutes.

## 6. Weekly Review

Every Friday:
> "This week I: [LIST ACCOMPLISHMENTS]. What did I avoid and why? Output: 1 thing to stop, 1 thing to start, 1 thing to continue. Also flag any risks I missed."

**Time saved:** 1 hour → 15 minutes.

## 7. Customer Feedback Synthesis

Paste 10-20 support tickets:
> "Synthesize these support tickets into: top 3 pain points, top 3 feature requests, and 1 quick win I can ship this week."

**Time saved:** 2 hours → 15 minutes.

## 8. Hiring Job Description

> "Write a job description for [ROLE] at [STAGE] startup. Include: core responsibilities, required skills, nice-to-have skills, how we work, and what success looks like in 90 days. Tone: direct, specific, no HR jargon."

**Time saved:** 3 hours → 20 minutes.

## 9. Competitive Analysis

> "Compare [COMPANY A] and [COMPANY B] on [DIMENSION]. Output as a table with columns: Feature | A | B | Winner | Why. Keep it scannable."

**Time saved:** 4 hours → 30 minutes.

## 10. Daily Prioritization

Every morning:
> "Here is my calendar and task list for today. Identify the 1-2 highest-leverage actions I should protect time for. Flag anything that can be delegated or deleted. Output: 3 bullet actions before 10 AM."

**Time saved:** 20 minutes → 2 minutes per day.
